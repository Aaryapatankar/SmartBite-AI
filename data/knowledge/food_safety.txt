FOOD SAFETY BASICS
Source note: summary of widely published public guidance (for example USDA FoodSafety.gov and the WHO "Five Keys to Safer Food"). Verify against the original sources for formal citations.

Cook chicken and other poultry until it is cooked through, with no pink meat and clear juices. Public guidance gives 74 C (165 F) as the safe internal temperature for poultry.
Fish should be cooked until the flesh turns opaque and flakes easily.
Eggs should be cooked until the white and yolk are firm. Avoid raw or undercooked eggs for young children, older adults, pregnant women and people with weak immunity.
Refrigerate cooked food within two hours. In hot weather (above 32 C) refrigerate within one hour.
Keep raw meat, fish and eggs separate from cooked food and vegetables. Use separate chopping boards or wash them well between uses.
Wash hands with soap before cooking and after handling raw meat.
Cooked rice left at room temperature for long periods can grow bacteria that cause food poisoning. Cool it quickly, refrigerate, and reheat until steaming hot.
Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot throughout. Do not reheat repeatedly.
Soak and fully cook dry kidney beans (rajma). Raw or undercooked kidney beans can cause illness.
