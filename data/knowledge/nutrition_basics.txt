NUTRITION BASICS
Source note: standard textbook values (Atwater general factors) and general public health guidance. Not personal medical advice.

Energy per gram: protein 4 kcal, carbohydrate 4 kcal, fat 9 kcal.
Protein: a commonly cited general guideline for healthy adults is about 0.8 g per kg of body weight per day. Needs can be higher for athletes or during recovery.
Good vegetarian protein sources in Indian cooking: paneer, dals (toor, moong, masoor), chickpeas, rajma, besan, curd, milk and soya chunks.
Good non-vegetarian protein sources: eggs, chicken and fish.
Combining cereals (rice, roti) with pulses (dal, rajma) gives a more complete amino acid mix.
Fibre comes from vegetables, whole grains and pulses and supports digestion and fullness.
Ghee and oil are calorie dense (about 9 kcal per gram). Measuring cooking oil is the easiest way to control calories in a curry.
Paneer is high in protein and fat. Using less oil and pairing it with vegetables such as spinach lowers the calories per serving.
People with diabetes, kidney disease or other conditions should consult a doctor or registered dietitian before changing their diet.
Nutrition values in this app are estimates calculated from approximate ingredient values and can differ from real food.
